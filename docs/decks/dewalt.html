Real photos · our park, our kids, our crew

Built by hand. For the kids who ride it.

It started in 2012 with one homemade ramp. In 2022 we opened Romania's largest indoor bikepark in Brașov, cut and screwed together by our own crew. Since then, kids have learned to ride, fall and get back up on these ramps: bikes, scooters and skateboards, with Action Camps every holiday.

×08 · The sportGuaranteed tough.
BMX Freestyle · Cheile GrădișteiREAL PHOTO

Backed by the Romanian Cycling Federation (FRC)

Romania's only UCI-standard BMX Freestyle park.

The park inside the hall is built to the UCI BMX Freestyle Park Guide: wooden ramps, safety zones, a real competition course. With the federation behind it, DEWALT Indoor Bikepark becomes the home of BMX Freestyle in Romania.

National

Romanian championships

National championships and cups for BMX Freestyle, organised with the FRC.

International

UCI C1 events

International competitions on the UCI calendar, with riders from abroad.

Pathway

From camp kid to rider

Kids start at Action Camps and train on the same course the champions ride.

Every competition here is held at DEWALT Indoor Bikepark. The name is in every result, photo, stream and article that names the venue.
