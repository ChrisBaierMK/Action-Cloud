The story only DEWALT can own

We don't buy our park. We build it.

Every ramp in Action Cloud was cut, screwed and shaped by our own crew. The move into the new hall is a full rebuild: 800 m² of plywood, hundreds of cuts, thousands of screws. With DEWALT tools in our hands, the build itself becomes the campaign.

"The Build" content series

  • Weekly build episodes on @action.cloud
  • Tool close-ups in real jobsite use
  • Crew and rider POV, from first cut to first drop-in
  • Opening-day film: "Built with DEWALT"

Where it lives after the build

  • Build wall inside the hall: "Built with DEWALT"
  • Every repair and new ramp, built on camera
  • Kids' camps: safe build workshops (supervised)
  • Every new zone in the next phase
