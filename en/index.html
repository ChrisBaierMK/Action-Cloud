Bucharest's kids need a place of their own. We're building it.

Romania's biggest action-sports park: 6,000 m² in north Bucharest where kids ride, jump, play and make friends, all year round. Help us open it.

Back the park Why it matters

  • About 6,000 m² in north Bucharest
  • Sport, food, shops and a place to stay under one roof
  • Open all year, indoors
  • Made by the crew that built Action Cloud Brașov

Action Cloud Bucharest gets thousands of kids off screens and moving, in a safe place, all year round.

Every sponsorship package means hours in which a kid moves, learns something new and has somewhere to go after school.

A meeting point
Kids make friends here face to face. They come for the ramps and stay for the crew.
Fun first
Sport kids choose for themselves, as the base for a healthy, active life, from the first ride to the podium.
Schools
PE lessons and class trips on weekday mornings at a school rate, with coached workshops.
Open to every kid
Bikes, scooters, boards and helmets to rent on site, plus a group card for youth clubs and after-school programmes.
A safe place all year
Supervised and indoors, open in winter and in the rain, instead of the street.
A path for talent
Action Camps and Action Cups take kids from beginner to competition level, coached by Romanian riders.

Why north Bucharest

  • Thousands of young families live nearby
  • Fast road to Ploiești and Brașov

We already built one. Now it moves to Bucharest and gets bigger.

  • 2022: opened in Brașov, Romania's largest indoor bikepark
  • Built to European level, by our own crew
  • Run by the Rosenau Cycling association
  • Sponsors so far: Rosenau Cycling, Techno Pro, Skates.ro
  • A model learned from parks across Europe: a non-profit association, sponsored by partners in return for visibility

Sponsor packages

A logo on a ramp is visibility. An open park is a place Bucharest's kids don't have yet. The park is being built like a funding campaign: partners back it, and every package gives them visibility. Your brand in front of kids aged 6–18, young riders and parents aged 30–45. Seven tiers, from 500 to 1,000,000 lei per year. Every tier comes with invitations to events and contests. From Feature partner up, you also get product placement in Action Cloud content.

    Your name on the supporter wall and on the website.

Tax benefit for sponsors

In Romania, sponsorships to an association listed in the RUC registry can generally be deducted from profit tax, up to the lower of 0.75% of turnover or 20% of the profit tax owed. Check the current rules with your accountant.
