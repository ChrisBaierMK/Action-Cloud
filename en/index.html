Every package includes invitations to events and Action Cups. From "Ramp partner" up, you also get product placement in clips. The Bucharest park is still a plan: visibility in the park starts when it opens, and we agree the contract period and what you get until then together.

Tax advantage for companies

In Romania, companies that pay profit tax can deduct sponsorships from the tax they owe, provided the association is listed in the register of entities eligible for tax deductions. The legal limit is the lower of 0.75% of turnover and 20% of the profit tax owed. Check the current rules and the association's listing with your accountant.
