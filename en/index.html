We already built one. Now it moves to Bucharest and gets bigger.

Action Cloud opened in Brașov in 2022 as Romania's largest indoor bikepark, built to European level. It's run by the Rosenau Cycling association, and the park was built with support from sponsors.

Partners so far: Rosenau Cycling, Techno Pro, Skates.ro.

We studied similar parks across Europe to see how they are built and funded. The model we follow is a non-profit association: partners sponsor the park and get visibility in return.

What's in the park

On wheels

    The skate and freestyle hall. The Send Room is the main line for MTB, BMX, scooter and skate. The Fun Room next door has a foam pit, a beginner jump line and a street section.

    One ramp into one giant airbag, shared by bikes, skis and snowboards, so riders train tricks all year round.

  • Concept image

    The mega ramp

    The showpiece of the park, built for pros, shows and Action Cups.

  • Concept image

    The bowl

    A bowl for skate, scooter and BMX.

Jump, climb and train

  • Concept image

    Trampoline hall

    A hall of trampolines with an airbag landing, for learning flips and spins before taking them to the ramps.

  • Concept image

    Parkour and street workout

    Boxes, walls and bars for running, vaulting and climbing, with a workout area beside them.

  • Concept image

    Functional and bodyweight training hall

    A pull-up rig, rings, ropes, kettlebells, barbells and jump boxes. For riders who want strength, and for parents who train while their kids are in the park.

  • Concept image

    Climbing wall and ninja warrior course

    For kids who don't ride: a wall to climb and an obstacle course to swing, hang and balance through.

  • Concept image

    Street-soccer cage and street ball court

    A caged pitch with a target wall for small-sided games, plus a street ball court.

For families

  • Concept image

  • Concept image

Programmes and events

  • Photo from Brașov

    School mornings and Action Camps

    School classes come on weekday mornings for PE lessons and class trips. Action Camps and courses run with partner coaches: MTB, scooter, skate, parkour, breakdance and yoga.

  • Photo from Brașov

    Action Cups and weekend live shows

    Regular competitions in every discipline in the park: MTB, BMX, scooter, skate, parkour, trampoline, street soccer and street ball. Age groups run from beginners to pros, with a season ranking and a final. On weekends, riders from every discipline put on live shows in one hall.

  • Concept image

    Getting in

    Online tickets, so there's no queue. Online parental consent, so older kids can come on their own. A live crowd level on the website, and clear safety rules, with helmets required on wheels. The skate, bike and scooter shops are by the entrance too.

Tax benefit for sponsors

In Romania, sponsorships to an association listed in the RUC registry can generally be deducted from profit tax, up to the lower of 0.75% of turnover or 20% of the profit tax owed. Check the current rules with your accountant.
