Tax advantage for companies

In Romania, companies that pay profit tax can deduct sponsorships to associations listed in the register of entities eligible for tax deductions, within the legal limit: the lower of 0.75% of turnover or 20% of the tax owed. Check the current rules and the association's listing with your accountant.
