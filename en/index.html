The park is planned, not built: these are estimates, not counts. An entry is anyone who comes through the door, parents included. Once open, we report the entries counted at the door.

How we estimated

We started from the visits per square metre that indoor action sports parks abroad report, and applied the cautious figure to our plan of about 6,000 m². There is no comparable hall in Romania, and nobody has counted how many people here ride. The first year is taken at about 70% of a settled year. About four in five entries are paying participants; the rest are accompanying adults. The split by age is an assumption, which we replace with ticket data six months after opening.

Until it opens
The Bucharest park is still a plan. Visibility in the park starts when it opens, and we agree together what you get until then.
The contract
It is signed with the non-profit Rosenau Cycling association. We agree the contract period and the payment currency together; prices are in euro.
Tax advantage for companies

In Romania, companies that pay profit tax can deduct sponsorships from the tax they owe, provided the association is listed in the register of entities eligible for tax deductions. The legal limit is the lower of 0.75% of turnover and 20% of the profit tax owed. Check the current rules and the association's listing with your accountant.

How do you want to join in?

Smaller is fine too. We build and run the park, you get a share of the takings. If the space belongs to someone else, leave your own details and talk to them first.

Action Crew: free. We email you when we announce the location and the opening date, so you are among the first to know. You can unsubscribe at any time. Under 16? Send the page to a parent.
