Built with our own hands.

Action Cloud took five years to build, with no outside money. Every ramp in the hall was cut, screwed and sanded by the people who ride it.

That's the whole idea. Nobody hands you the park, the trick or the life you want. You start building, you get scared, you commit anyway.

The site's never finished. Neither are we.

    Where it started

    Action Cloud opened in Brașov in 2022: 1,200 m² of indoor riding for BMX, mountain bike, scooter and skate, split into the Send Room and the Fun Room. Kids aged 7 to 16 came for Action Camp, riders came to train, and the whole scene showed up for Action Cup, Christmas Jam and Trick or Treat.
