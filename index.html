We already built one. Now it moves to Bucharest and gets bigger.

Action Cloud opened in Brașov in 2022 as Romania's largest indoor bikepark, built to European level. It's run by the Rosenau Cycling association, and the park was built with support from sponsors.

We run Action Camps for kids: bike and scooter camps.

What's inside

The model is already proven in Switzerland. Skills Park Winterthur fills 6,000 m² of a former factory with action sports, and on a busy day it hosts around 2,000 kids. Action Cloud Bucharest brings the same concept to Romania.

Action zones

  • Skate and freestyle hall: the Send Room line (MTB, BMX, scooter, skate) and the Fun Room (foam pit, beginner jump line, street section)
  • Indoor pumptrack
  • Trampoline hall with airbag landing
  • Parkour and street workout area
  • Street-soccer cage with target wall, plus a street ball court

Signature features

  • Big airbag jump shared by bikes, skis and snowboards: one ramp into one giant airbag, so riders train tricks all year round
  • Mega ramp: the showpiece for pros, shows and Action Cups
  • Bowl for skate, scooter and BMX
  • Climbing wall and ninja warrior course, for kids who don't ride
  • Weekend live shows with riders from every discipline in one hall

Mini Action

  • A toddler zone with balance bikes, mini ramps and soft play, so the youngest kids and their parents have a place too

Programmes

  • School classes on weekday mornings: PE lessons and class trips
  • Action Camps and courses with partner coaches: MTB, scooter, skate, parkour, breakdance, yoga
  • Kids' birthday parties in 2–3 party rooms running at the same time

Action Cups

  • Regular competitions in every discipline in the park: MTB, BMX, scooter, skate, parkour, trampoline, street soccer and street ball
  • Age groups from beginners to pros, with a season ranking and a final

Around the park

  • Bistro and café with a weekly lunch menu and a small menu, looking into the park
  • Gaming room
  • Skate, bike and scooter shops

Visitor system

  • Online tickets, so there's no queue
  • Online parental consent, so older kids can come on their own
  • Live crowd level on the website
  • Clear safety rules, with helmets required on wheels

Why north Bucharest

  • Thousands of young families live nearby
  • Fast road to Ploiești and Brașov

Tax benefit for sponsors

In Romania, sponsorships to an association listed in the RUC registry can generally be deducted from profit tax, up to the lower of 0.75% of turnover or 20% of the profit tax owed. Check the current rules with your accountant.
