Built with our own hands

It started in 2012 with one ramp in a barn. Then a backyard full of quarter pipes, then a wall of timber frames in an empty hall. In 2023 it was a full indoor bikepark, built with no outside money by the riders who use it.

The site has never been finished. Neither are we.

    The park

    The only indoor bikepark in Romania built to European level. A safe place to train through the cold months, for mountain bike, BMX, scooter, inline and skate.

    Send Room

    A medium line for mountain bike, scooter and inline, plus a lounge for riders and spectators.

    Fun Room

    A foam pit for trying new tricks safely, a small jump line for beginners and a street section.

    Events

    Action Cup, Christmas Jam and Trick or Treat. Riders come from all over the country.

    Action Camp

    Bike and scooter camps for kids aged 7 to 16, so they progress safely in urban sports.
