Inside Action Cloud, Brașov

Scared is fine. Stuck isn't.

I've been on a bike for 27 years. Downhill, slopestyle, dirt jump, backflips. National champion at 17. Zero broken bones.

Not because I'm brave. I get scared like everyone. The difference is I stopped waiting for the fear to leave. It doesn't. You decide anyway, and you commit.

That works on a jump line, and it works on everything else: the business you keep putting off, the move, the trip, the conversation. Action Cloud started as a park. It's become the way I try to live.

Chris Baier, founder

    Action Cloud is an indoor bikepark in Brașov for BMX, mountain bike, scooter and skate. Rain stops nobody. Kids aged 7 to 16 come for Action Camp, riders come to train, and the whole scene shows up for Action Cup, Christmas Jam and Trick or Treat.
